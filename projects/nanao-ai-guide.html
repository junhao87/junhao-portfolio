04 / THE IDEA

Travel is more
than a map.

What if a journey could remember how you felt, not only where you went?

Most tourism applications are organized around destinations, directions, attractions, and itineraries. MindScape Travel explores an emotion-centered alternative: travelers reflect on their experiences, AI helps interpret the emotional tone, and those insights become the starting point for creative memories of place.

The project connects Nan'ao's cultural tourism context with digital storytelling and visual design, with a goal of encouraging richer connections between people, destinations, and local culture.
