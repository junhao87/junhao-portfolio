01 / THE IDEA

Preserving a traditional art form through a new creative interface.

Batik Studio is an interactive AI-powered web application I independently researched and developed. It combines traditional Malaysian Batik motifs, generative AI, real-time 3D fashion visualization, and multilingual cultural education in one creative platform.

The aim is not to replace traditional craftsmanship. It is to make Batik design more accessible to younger generations, designers, and cultural enthusiasts while highlighting the meaning behind its motifs.

02 / DESIGN CHALLENGE

Craft heritage.
Digital possibilities.

Traditional Batik creation involves specialized knowledge and techniques. Conventional digital tools often provide pattern editing without contextual cultural education, AI-assisted motif combination, or a way to preview designs on garments.

I set out to create a single interactive flow that connects motif selection, AI-assisted exploration, color customization, 3D clothing previews, and the stories behind Batik patterns.

“How might technology help people discover, experiment with, and appreciate Batik without losing its cultural context?”
03 / THE PLATFORM

One platform.

01 / GENERATE
✳

Hybrid AI pattern generation

Choose Batik A and Batik B. Poisson Image Blending merges the motifs; Stable Diffusion 1.5 Img2Img refines the result. Each run returns five candidate designs.

02 / VISUALIZE
◇

Interactive 3D fashion

Preview patterns on a T-shirt and Baju Kurung. Rotate garments and tune texture scale, repetition, rotation, and offsets. Top and bottom textures can be edited separately.

03 / CUSTOMIZE
◌

Color and design controls

Apply curated color palettes and HSL-based recoloring that aims to retain key motif contours and structural details.

04 / DISCOVER
✧

Batik Kon cultural assistant

Explore Batik history, motif origins, symbolism, and usage through a structured, rule-based knowledge base in English, Mandarin, and Bahasa Melayu, including voice narration.

05 / REVISIT
▤

Saved design history

Keep and reload patterns, palette choices, texture settings, and design metadata with browser-based IndexedDB storage.

07 / EXPERIMENTATION

Built. Compared.
Evaluated.

Research guided the final design decisions rather than treating generation as a black box.

Five AI approaches evaluated

  1. Stable Diffusion XL + T2I-Adapter
  2. Naive blending + Img2Img
  3. ControlNet + Canny edge guidance
  4. ControlNet + color constraints
  5. Poisson blending + Stable Diffusion Img2Img ↗ selected

The selected method offered the strongest qualitative balance of motif preservation, visual coherence, color control, and creative variation in the project's comparison.

Reproducible inference setup

Aligned model and scheduler settings across local GPU and Google Colab environments, including random seeds, DDIM, FP16 precision, software versions, and model configuration.

These matched environments produced consistent generation results in the project's cross-platform tests.
