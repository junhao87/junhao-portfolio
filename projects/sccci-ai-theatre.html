02 / THE OBJECTIVE

Institutional history,
made accessible.

How do you turn a century of trusted historical material into an experience visitors can actually explore?

SCCCI's commemorative experience needed a way for visitors to ask about its history, milestones, contributions and cultural heritage in both English and Chinese. A general-purpose chatbot alone could provide answers that were fluent but not necessarily grounded in the institution's source material.

The AI assistant therefore prioritized curated SCCCI knowledge by combining structured frequently asked questions with document retrieval, giving visitors a clearer path to source-informed answers.

WHY HYBRID?

Structured FAQs keep common institutional answers predictable. RAG extends coverage when visitors ask questions beyond the prepared FAQ set. Both paths are designed to favor supported content over unsupported speculation.

WHAT WAS TESTED

Retrieval and response behavior

The development version includes 25 English and 25 Chinese FAQ entries. Testing focused on routing FAQ versus RAG questions, language handling, relevant references, and answers involving SCCCI-specific terminology.

WHAT WAS REFINED

Reliability before delivery

Refinement included similarity thresholds, keyword and exact-term lookup, grounded prompts, topic handling, answer length and source display. No quantitative model accuracy metric is claimed here.
